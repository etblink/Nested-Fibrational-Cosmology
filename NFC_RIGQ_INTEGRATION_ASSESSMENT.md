# RIG-Q Integration Assessment

*Produced L+49 on operator request, following the reading of the
synthesis note "NFC – Lawful Representation" (443 pp). An honest
evaluation of Project RIG-Q (Canonical Forcing of Representation and
Induced Metric) against the corpus's promotion and status discipline
(Book VII promotion law and status-inflation rules; Book V legitimacy;
import discipline), to determine what — if anything — is canonically
promotable. Assessment only: no obligation status changed, nothing
promoted.*

**Verdict in one line:** RIG-Q is **not canonically promotable** — every
load-bearing result is an open theorem shell, its [C] corollaries are
conditional on those open shells, and its later "provisionally hardened"
status is non-canonical and rests in part on a governance rule rather
than a proof. It is a **legitimate, well-structured prospective research
program** that correctly targets a real seam (discharging the (SI)
hypothesis of Book VI's Lorentzian-signature theorem), and it belongs in
the Speculative Holding register as such, with its obligations
catalogued.

---

## 1. What RIG-Q is, and what it correctly targets

RIG-Q describes itself accurately: "not a new physical branch ... a
bridge-hardening packet spanning the YM encoding stack, the Book VI
continuum interface, and the GR metric realization layer," at "CERT-PROJ
at most." Its aim is to convert Book VI's emergent-geometry posture from
*lawful-once-chosen* to *canonically-forced-by-source*.

The target is real and accurately identified. Book VI
**thm:lorentzian-signature [C]** is genuinely conditional on hypothesis
(SI): the quadratic form Q is taken **nondegenerate by hypothesis** and
**cone-null compatibility is assumed**. RIG-Q's program is to discharge
exactly these — derive Q canonically from source data (so it is not
"given"), prove nondegeneracy from source rigidity, and derive cone-null
compatibility as a theorem. That is a legitimately-aimed discharge
program against a correctly-identified open hypothesis.

## 2. Promotion status of RIG-Q's results — assessed against Book VII

**The load-bearing results are open theorem shells.** RQ.5 (source
pre-inner-product), RQ.6 (universal realization), RQ.7 (unitary
uniqueness), RQ.9 (induced-form invariance), RQ.11 (nondegeneracy),
RQ.12 (metric identification bridge), RQ.14 (cone-null compatibility),
RQ.15 (derived Lorentz signature) are all tagged **[O]** by the document
itself. Open shells are proof targets, not proofs; by Book VII nothing
[O] is canonical.

**The [C] corollaries are conditional on the open shells, not on proved
hypotheses.** RQ.8, RQ.13, RQ.16 read "assuming Theorems RQ.5–RQ.7 /
RQ.9–RQ.12 / RQ.11–RQ.14." In the corpus, [C] means *conditionally
established modulo declared hypotheses that are themselves proved or
licensed structure*. A corollary conditional on **unproved [O]
theorems** is not [C] in that sense — it is [O]-dependent, and inherits
the open status of its antecedents. It cannot be promoted while its
antecedents are open.

**Result: no RIG-Q result meets the promotion law.**
thm:promotion-requires-transfer requires a soundly scoped certificate of
a proved result plus a transfer theorem. RIG-Q has neither for any
load-bearing claim — it has a proof *program*, which is a plan to
someday obtain certificates, not a certificate.

## 3. The "provisionally hardened / provisionally closed" claims — the critical issue

The document's later sections upgrade the main chain to "provisionally
closed / provisionally hardened," and state of at least one key theorem
that its "force comes from the project rule" of no-hidden-
renormalization "as part of canon governance." Under the corpus's
status discipline this does **not** survive:

- **"Provisionally hardened" is not a canonical status tag.** The canon
  recognizes [U], [C], [D], [O], [R], [B]. "Provisionally
  hardened/closed" is project-internal bookkeeping, not a corpus status;
  translated into canonical tags, a result whose proof is incomplete and
  whose antecedents are open reads as **[O]**.
- **Force-from-a-governance-rule is status inflation, not proof.** A
  theorem whose force derives from accepting a project rule rather than
  from a proof is exactly what Book VII's status-inflation section and
  cor:conditional-no-unconditional guard against. At most such a result
  is [C] conditional on the rule being adopted as an **axiom** — and
  adding an axiom is a major architectural decision, not a hardening.
- **The document concedes the point.** Its own governing rule states the
  provisionally-closed chain "may not be silently upgraded to fully
  discharged while the named bridges remain open." Read honestly, the
  main chain is open shells with a proposed strategy — which is precisely
  the document's *early* framing ("theorem shells, not false claims of
  completion"). The tension is between that honest early framing and the
  later "provisionally closed" language; under canonical tags, the early
  framing is the correct one.

## 4. The load-bearing hinge, and gating on open GR obligations

**O-RQ.1 (source pre-inner-product extraction) is the make-or-break
hinge.** Can a canonical pre-inner-product be *extracted* from source
transport/spectral data (a Gram rule K(x,y)) rather than chosen? The
document proposes the free-module → source-sesquilinear-form → quotient
→ completion route, and honestly flags (Frontier RQ.F1, RQ.F2) that the
source data may be insufficient and uniqueness may fail outright. This
is open, and it is the foundation the entire chain stands on.

**The chain is additionally gated on already-open GR obligations.** The
metric-identification bridge (O-RQ.6 / RQ.12) welds Q_can to GR's
g_{μν}, but GR's metric existence is itself open: **O-GR.real [O]**
(existence of g_{μν} on the development) and **O-GR.compat [O]**
(metric/connection + EFE structure). RIG-Q cannot weld to an object
whose existence is an open GR obligation; the weld presupposes a
discharge the corpus has not made.

**Consequence for the L+46 frontier verdict: unchanged.** The Metric
rung was assessed out-of-toolkit at L+46 because it requires
conditionally-derived Book VI geometry. RIG-Q is precisely a *proposed
program to attack that frontier* — built on an open extraction hinge and
gated on open GR obligations — not a crossing of it. It sharpens the
roadmap; it does not move the boundary.

## 5. To RIG-Q's credit (what the assessment affirms)

- It is **internally anti-smuggling-disciplined**: Standing Rules RQ.1–3
  (no representation smuggling, representation/metric separation, no
  hidden upgrade across the weld) mirror the corpus's own discipline and
  are exactly the right governing rules for this seam.
- It **correctly diagnoses the open hypotheses** of
  thm:lorentzian-signature and the parallel-geometry problem (Book VI's
  Q vs GR's g_{μν}), both of which the corpus already tracks.
- It is **honestly tagged at the shell level** and declares its failure
  frontiers (RQ.F1–F4) rather than hiding them.

These make RIG-Q a high-quality prospective program — which is precisely
why it should be recorded as a program, not promoted as results.

## 6. Disposition

- **Canonical promotion: NO**, on three independent grounds: all
  load-bearing results are [O] shells (§2); the [C] corollaries are
  conditional on those open shells (§2); the "provisionally hardened"
  status is non-canonical and partly governance-rule-forced (§3). The
  chain is further gated on the open hinge O-RQ.1 and open GR
  obligations O-GR.real/O-GR.compat (§4).
- **Legitimate role: a prospective research program** recorded in the
  Speculative Holding register, with its endpoint target, eight closure
  obligations (O-RQ.1 → O-RQ.8), and four failure frontiers (RQ.F1–F4)
  catalogued. This mirrors how NFR is held: a pre-canonical instrument,
  valued for its structure, promoted to nothing.
- **Status-translation caveat (recorded for any future mining):** RIG-Q's
  internal "provisionally closed/hardened" statuses must be translated
  to canonical tags before any corpus contact; under that translation
  the main chain reads [O], and the no-hidden-renormalization "hardening"
  reads as either [C]-conditional-on-an-unadopted-axiom or [O]. Importing
  the internal statuses verbatim would be status inflation.
- **No new corpus [O] obligations** are created: RIG-Q's obligations are
  prospective-program obligations in the holding register, not
  corpus-canonical [O] tags — exactly as the NFR assessment added none.
  The corpus [O] census is unchanged.

**Net:** RIG-Q correctly identifies and roadmaps the discharge of Book
VI's Lorentzian-signature (SI) hypothesis and the Q–g weld, but proves
none of it; every load-bearing step is open or gated on open
obligations, and its "hardened" status is non-canonical. It is recorded
as a prospective program in Speculative Holding. The L+42/L+46 verdict
stands: the Metric/representation/Lorentz-signature frontier remains
out-of-toolkit, with RIG-Q now its most developed roadmap.

*Assessment only. No status changed; nothing promoted. RIG-Q recorded as
a prospective program in the Speculative Holding register.*
